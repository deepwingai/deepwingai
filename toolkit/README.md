## How to use · 使用方法

Fill the Checklist item by item. Determinations follow Chapter 13 of the framework: Pass where records exist to prove it, Partial pass where it rests on habit or word of mouth, Fail where there is none or it is unclear. The workbook adds no scores or weights.

逐项填写检查表。判定按框架第 13 章：有记录能证明为合格，靠习惯或口头为部分合格，没有或不清楚为不合格。工具包不另设分数或权重。

The three cross-layer gates (Gate 1 Gateway, Gate 9 Platform, Gate 10 Alignment & Autonomy) carry no layer check items and are assessed under Chapter 12.

三道跨层关口（第 1 关网关、第 9 关平台、第 10 关统一与自主）不设层检查项，按第 12 章评估。

Check item text is copied from the framework, sections 5.6 to 11.6 of each edition. Where this workbook and the framework differ, the framework governs. The workbook version follows the framework version.

检查项原文取自框架两个版本的 5.6 至 11.6 节。与框架不一致之处，以框架为准。工具包版本随框架版本。
