# Changelog

Version history for this repository and for the public assets it mirrors. Dates are the dates of the underlying publication or export, in ISO format.

## 2026-11-09

- `toolkit/`: DW-AI-ARCH Evaluation Workbook v1.0 added, English and Chinese editions. The 70 check items of Formal Edition v1.0 (sections 5.6 to 11.6 of each edition) as a fill-in checklist with summary, evidence register and risk register, plus the items as CSV. Determinations follow Chapter 13; no scores or weights are added.

## 2026-10-31

Repository contents prepared for first publication (account registration pending). Initial contents:

- `framework/DW-AI-ARCH-v1.0.md`: full text of the DeepWing Framework formal edition v1.0, English edition and Chinese edition in one file. 385 numbered clauses and 70 check items per edition.
- `data/incident-cases-public.csv`: first public data export, the Desk 1 incident case register public set as published on the website on this date: 53 cases (INC-001 to INC-053) plus the special volume SP-001. Fields and conventions are documented in `data/README.md`.
- `README.md`, `LICENSE` (CC BY 4.0), and this changelog.

## 2026-10-27

- DW-AI-ARCH Formal Edition v1.0 ratified and published on the website (deepwingai.com/framework). Deposit copy archived on Zenodo, DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23715226 (all-versions DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23715225). The framework count basis at deposit was the public case set as it stood on that date; the register has since grown, and register growth is tracked on the website and in the data exports here.
